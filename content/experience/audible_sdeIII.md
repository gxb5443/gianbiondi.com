### SDE III

Audible is the world's largest producer and provider of audiobooks and audio entertainment, reaching millions of listeners globally as an Amazon company.

I joined Audible's Consumer Domains org as an SDE III, where I work on the credits system, the core mechanism that powers Audible's membership model and allows members to acquire titles. The credits system sits at the heart of how customers interact with Audible's catalog, making it one of the most critical and high-traffic domains in the consumer experience.
