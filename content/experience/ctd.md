### First Software Job

My first real software job. Creative Technology Designs was a small, local company
which developed mobile apps for other small businesses, like bowling alleys and car washes.
It featured a common app base which was white labeled and published to the app stores.
It also featured a web-based CRM so business owners could configure and update their
app content themselves. I did general technology work like making simple websites, developing
the apps, and working on more custom or bespoke content. The company went out of business in 
September 2013.
